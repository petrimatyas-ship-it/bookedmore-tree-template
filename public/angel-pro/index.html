FAQ

Questions customers ask.

Do you repair garage doors you didn't install?

Yes. We repair all major brands of residential and commercial garage doors and openers, no matter who installed them.

Can you build a custom gate to match my house?

Yes. We design and fabricate gates in steel, iron and wood, swing or sliding, and can match an existing fence or style.

Can you add an opener to the gate I already have?

In most cases, yes. We look at the gate's size, weight and hinges, then recommend the right operator and access control.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, measure, and give you a clear price before any work starts. No pressure.

Can I send photos for a faster estimate?

Yes. Add photos in the quote form below or send them in the chat, and we can often give you a ballpark the same day.

Do you work on commercial and industrial doors?

Yes. We install and repair overhead and rolling steel doors for shops, warehouses and loading docks.

How long does a new gate take?

It depends on the design. Repairs are usually quick, while custom-fabricated gates are built to order. We give you a timeline with the estimate.

How can I pay?

Ask us when you get your estimate and we'll go over the payment options for your job.
