Our specialty

Ceramic coating, done properly

A liquid polymer that bonds with your paint. Once cured, it shields the vehicle from UV damage, oxidation, dirt, road grime and chemical stains. Utah's road salt, dust and harsh sun age paint faster than most owners realize.

HydrophobicWater beads and rolls off
UV resistantProtects against fading
Scratch resistantA line of defense for paint
Stain resistantChemicals and grime wipe off

Others up-charge, add fees and do half the job. Every Apex coating covers the full exterior, not just the paint, with no extra fees for windows or rims, and every coating is backed by a warranty.

FAQ

Good questions

Do you need access to water or power?

No. On the van we have both power and water, so you just leave the vehicle open and that's all.

How long does a detail take?

Depending on the car and the package chosen, from 1 hour to 4 hours. If you want focused or quick, we do it all.

What areas do you serve?

Kearns, Taylorsville, West Valley, West Jordan, Murray, Herriman and surrounding areas. Anywhere past our 10-mile limit costs more.

What forms of payment do you take?

Debit, credit, Cash App, Venmo, Zelle and cash.

How do I look after a ceramic coating?

Curing usually takes 1 to 7 days. After that, just watch out for automatic car washes. A hose and a rag is all it needs, or get a bundle for a discount and months of washes with Apex.

Can you build a custom package?

Yes. George reaches out to get your vehicle info and confirms your custom package and final price before any work begins.
